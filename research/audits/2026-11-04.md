## Findings
1. **apexpm/registry.py `mode_for` (~l.108)**: with DRY_RUN=false and PAPER=false, a strategy whose status is `paper` got mode "PAPER", but `Broker` builds the Alpaca client with `paper=cfg.paper`, so the order went to the LIVE endpoint. Violates GOVERNANCE ("paper: Paper only"). `ExampleBreakout` is registered `paper` in loop.py. Fix: applied (see below).
2. **tests/test_db.py:32 `test_rejections_and_state`** failed: `rejections.session_date` is the ET date, the test queried the UTC date; fails every day between 00:00 UTC and ~04:00/05:00 UTC (it failed at this run, 00:33 UTC). Fix: applied (use `et_date()`).
3. **apexpm/profiles.py:~108 (`positions_exception_on`)**: `max(self.max_positions, MAX_POSITIONS_HARD)` lifts the cap to 5 in every band, including hyper (<$1k) whose rule is one position at a time. One tap on the dashboard defeats the hyper one-position limit. Proposed fix: ignore the exception when `rules["max_positions"] == 1`.
4. **apexpm/loop.py:84-98, 126-141**: the kill-switch anchor (`starting_equity`) is set at `startup()` only; `_roll_day` does not re-anchor, so a bot running multiple days measures drawdown from the first day's equity (and `state.halted` is never cleared at roll). Docs say "session's starting equity". Proposed fix: re-read/seed the per-day anchor in `_roll_day`; decide whether a halt should persist across days.
5. **apexpm/loop.py:249 and :92 (reset detection)**: equity >50% away from the anchor with no realized P&L today and no open trades is treated as a paper reset and re-anchored, in live as well as paper. An overnight/gap loss >50% on held positions would be absorbed rather than halted. Low likelihood (flat-by exits) but the check is not paper-only. Proposed fix: restrict to `cfg.paper`.
6. **apexpm/loop.py:605, 719 (exit price fallback)**: with no price available a stop/target exit is recorded at the plan price, flattering the journal (no slippage). Only when feed is down; informational.
7. **apexpm/loop.py:688 (option runner steps)**: `t["entry"] * 0.5` used as ATR and hard-coded +50% steps ignore `opt_premium_target`; harmless now, drifts if the config changes.
8. Docs: SPEC.md and research/ (README, studies) do not exist in this repo, so SPEC security checkpoints/hard rules and research-claim consistency could not be audited.

Checked, no issue: all Broker/OptionsBroker write paths refuse under DRY_RUN; research/suspended/retired map to DRY_RUN and are excluded from `active()`; `shadow_only` crypto never reaches `_enter`; stream callbacks catch exceptions and the loop catches per-symbol/per-trade errors; backtest fills at the next bar's open, HTF uses completed bars only, EMA prior-bar lookups OK; no secrets, key patterns or /Users/ paths in tracked files (`.env` untracked, `.env.example` placeholders).
